Actuator lines for the actuator line model (ALM): blade-element geometry, rigid-body kinematics and aerodynamic loads.

Ported from Incompact3d's acl_elem.f90 and acl_utils.f90. An actuator line is defined by its stations (element ends) on the quarter-chord line; element centres, unit vectors (spanwise s, chordwise t, normal n), areas and chords are derived from them by make_line_geometry. Loads come from the static airfoil polars, with , and the normal and chordwise components of the fluid velocity relative to the element.

The dynamic-stall, added-mass, random-walk and constant-circulation options of Incompact3d are not ported.
