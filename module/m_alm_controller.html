Variable-speed generator-torque controller and PI collective blade-pitch controller of the NREL offshore 5 MW baseline turbine, as written by J. Jonkman (NREL/NWTC) for the IEA Annex XXIII OC3 studies (DISCON).

Ported from Incompact3d's acl_controller.f90. The torque law has five regions (1, 1 1/2, 2, 2 1/2 and 3) on the low-pass filtered generator speed; the pitch controller is a gain-scheduled PI on the speed error, with pitch-angle and pitch-rate limits. The pitch gains and limits are those of the NREL 5 MW turbine; the torque parameters come from the turbine input file.
