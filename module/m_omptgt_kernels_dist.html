Distributed compact-scheme kernels for the OpenMP target backend, following cuda/kernels/distributed.f90.

A CUDA kernel there runs one thread per pencil line, (i, b) = (threadIdx, blockIdx), each sweeping its line serially. Here every kernel offloads the same (b, i) iteration space with teams distribute parallel do and hands each line to a declare target routine ending in _line. Keeping the sweep in its own routine makes all of its temporaries per-line locals with no private clause to keep in step, and the explicit distribute parallel do keeps NVHPC from serialising each team, as it does when teams loop meets a procedure call.

Arrays are explicit-shape or assumed-size dummies named in OMP_DEVICE_ADDR (see m_omptgt_common). Fields are (SZ, n, n_groups), halo buffers (SZ, 4, n_groups) and the 2x2-system buffers (SZ, 1, n_groups). The boundary stencils are 9 wide (n_halo = 4).

Cyclic pentadiagonal solve via Sherman-Morrison-Woodbury (rank 4): A_cyc = A_np + UC with U = [e_1|e_2|e_{n-1}|e_n]. z holds A_np^{-1}U (see omptgt_tdsops_t%penta_z_dev); each line forms M = I + CZ, solves Mc = Cy and applies y -= Zc.
