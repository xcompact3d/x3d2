Horizontal-axis wind turbines for the actuator line model (ALM).

Ported from Incompact3d's acl_turb.f90 and the turbine half of acl_model.f90. A turbine owns its blades and optional tower (each an actuator_line_t), its airfoil polars, and its rotor state (azimuth, angular velocity, collective pitch, controller). This module is free of fields and MPI: the flow velocity at the actuator points is sampled and the forces are projected by m_alm.

A turbine is described by a &TurbineSpecs namelist file (.turb), in the same format as Incompact3d. Supported options: - TypeFlag = 1 (horizontal axis) - OperFlag = 1 (constant rotation, omega = ureftsr/R), 2 (NREL 5 MW torque and pitch controller) or 4 (omega = U_upstreamtsr/R from an upstream probe) - RotFlag = 1 (clockwise) or 2 (counterclockwise) - towerFlag = 0/1, EndEffectsFlag = 0 (none), 1 (Glauert) or 2 (Shen et al. 2005) with TipCorr/RootCorr OperFlag = 3 (list controller), AddedMassFlag, DynStallFlag, RandomWalkForcingFlag, ConstantCirculationFlag and AeroElastFlag are not ported and stop the run when set.
