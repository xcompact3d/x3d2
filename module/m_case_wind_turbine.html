Case for wind-turbine simulations (the two_turbines setup): uniform inflow and convective outflow in x; y and z each either periodic or bounded by free-slip walls (BC = 'neumann', 'neumann', Incompact3d ncl = 1). Incompact3d's two_turbines example has walls in y and z.

The case owns a polymorphic turbine forcing model (turbine_model_t), set up by m_turbine_setup from the iturbine switch. It drives the model from its forcings()/postprocess() hooks.

The inflow/outflow boundary treatment mirrors m_case_cylinder.

public function wall_weights(mesh, dir, walls) result(w)

Weights of the local vertices of direction dir in the outlet flow rate, as the discrete divergence sees it: its interpolation along a non-periodic direction gives the global boundary planes half the weight of the others (trapezoid). u is not stamped on a free-slip wall, so the wall planes keep their 1/2.

public subroutine define_BC_wind_turbine(self)

Boundary Conditions hook (called per substep before transeq). Builds the inlet Dirichlet profile only; outflow parameters are computed in apply_BC from the post-integration velocity. u = bc_start_u + inlet_noise(1) * um * (2r - 1) v = bc_start_v + inlet_noise(2) * um * (2r - 1) w = bc_start_w + inlet_noise(3) * um * (2r - 1)

public subroutine apply_BC_wind_turbine(self, u, v, w)

Pre-correction (called per substep after the integrator step), following Incompact3d's pre_correc: 1. cx = uxmaxgdt/dx from the post-integration field. 2. Dirichlet inflow at x=1 and convective outflow at x=nx for u,v,w: u(nx) = u(nx) - cx(u(nx) - u(nx-1)), with a uniform shift of u on the outlet that makes the flow rate out equal the flow rate in. Incompact3d balances the plain means over the planes; here the planes are weighted as the discrete divergence sees them (see wall_weights), the same thing for periodic y and z, and exact with walls, where the plain mean leaves a uniform divergence the Neumann-x projection cannot remove. 3. Free-slip walls: v = 0 on the y walls and w = 0 on the z walls. The tangential components take the even-symmetry closure, and the Neumann pressure BC gives no correction on the wall planes.
