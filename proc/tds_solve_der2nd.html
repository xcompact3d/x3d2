public subroutine tds_solve_der2nd(self, d2u, u, tdsops_d2, tdsops_d1)

Second derivative of u on a possibly stretched grid. On a stretched grid d2u/dy2 = vert_ds2d2u/dxi2 + vert_d2s(vert_ds*du/dxi), but tds_solve with a second derivative operator applies only the vert_ds2 factor. This solves the first derivative with tdsops_d1, the second derivative with tdsops_d2, and adds the stretch_correct term of tdsops_d2 times the first derivative.
