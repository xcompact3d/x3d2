public subroutine stretching_matrix(self, geo, xdirps, ydirps, zdirps, n_blk, blk_st)

Stretching necessitates a special operation in spectral space. The coefficients for the operation are stored in matrix form. The matrices and trans_ cover the spectral block of size n_blk at offset blk_st, the local spectral pencil by default. trans_ hold the modes of the block and take the local index.

Ref. JCP 228 (2009), 5989–6015, Sec 5
