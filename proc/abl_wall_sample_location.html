public subroutine abl_wall_sample_location(mesh, dsampling, sample_plane, sample_weight, sampling_height)

Where the ABL wall model samples the velocity, dsampling grid spacings above the floor (vertex 1). As in Incompact3d's wall_sgs_noslip, the sample is interpolated linearly between the vertices below and above that height, so a non-integer dsampling is honoured rather than rounded to a grid plane.
