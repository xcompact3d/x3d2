public subroutine exec_dist_tds_compact(du, u, u_recv_s, u_recv_e, du_send_s, du_send_e, du_recv_s, du_recv_e, tdsops, par, dir, n_groups)

Distributed compact solve along dir. The reduced system that couples the ranks is solved with the neighbour-only 2x2 approximation, or exactly where tdsops%dist_exact asks for it (see m_dist_reduced). The exact solve's right-hand sides, two planes per field, go through the host.
