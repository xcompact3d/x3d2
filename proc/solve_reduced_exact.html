public subroutine solve_reduced_exact(x_s, x_e, d_s, d_e, sa_1, sc_1, sa_n, sc_n, par, dir)

Solve the reduced system of every line exactly. Line l of d_s and d_e must be the same physical line on every rank along dir, as it is for the neighbour exchange of the default second phase.
