public function spectral_plus_mirror_bytes(ctx, num_gpus) result(nbytes8)

The spectral slab plus the BC specific extra terms: the mirror buffers of 100 and of unstretched 010 at num_gpus>1, the second array and the transposed workspace of 110, and the Poisson coefficient matrices of 010 with y stretching.
