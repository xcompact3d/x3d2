public function fold_destination(i, n) result(dst)

Where entry i of a non-periodic line of n points lands when the line is gathered by fold_index: the inverse permutation. The odd entries fill the first half in order, the even entries the second half in reverse. Used where a kernel moves the whole line in one pass and the fold rides along with it, as the Z fold of the 111 case does.
