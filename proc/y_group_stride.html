public function y_group_stride(self) result(stride)

Groups between successive z in the OMP DIR_X ordering, group = stride*(z - 1) + y_block. It is the padded y-block count (see get_index_dir in m_ordering), so it can exceed the number of y-blocks a field uses, e.g. a cell field on a non-periodic y.
