public subroutine apply_spatial_filter(self)

Apply the explicit low-pass filter to the velocity, as Incompact3d does for wall-modelled ABL runs (ifilter=1, C_filter).

Compact schemes cannot dissipate the 2dx mode and the staggered pressure projection cannot see it, yet the collocated derivatives in the skew-symmetric convection do. Left alone that mode drives the collocated divergence away from zero, and the udiv(u) half of the convection then acts as a spurious momentum source.
