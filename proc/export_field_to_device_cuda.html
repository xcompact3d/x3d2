public subroutine export_field_to_device_cuda(self, buffer, f, dims, to_sp)

DIR_X fields are reordered and unpadded by a single kernel. DIR_C fields are unpadded directly, and DIR_Y/DIR_Z fields are reordered to DIR_C first. Precision conversion happens in the same kernel.
