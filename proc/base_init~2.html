public subroutine base_init(self, mesh, xdirps, ydirps, zdirps, n_spec, n_sp_st, n_blk, blk_st)

Sets up the state every case shares. The backend's init calls it once it has set bc_code, which the spectral extents it passes in already depend on. The supported BC codes are the cases of the backend factories, create_omp_poisson_fft and create_cuda_poisson_fft. n_blk and blk_st are passed on to stretching_matrix.
