public subroutine fft_postprocess_110_omp(self)

Post-process div U* in spectral space for the non-periodic x and y case. After the (nx, ny, nz) -> (nz, nx, ny) transpose applied by fft_forward_110_omp, dim1 holds the periodic z r2c modes, dim2 holds the non-periodic x modes and dim3 holds the non-periodic y modes.

The z-pencil of sp (which c_x is) splits dim2 (x modes) across p_col, so the X paired split and recombine, which each couple a mode with its mirror in dim2, hop to the y-pencil of sp (where dim2 is whole) and back, the same way fft_postprocess_100_omp does. The Y paired split/solve/recombine run directly on the z-pencil, where dim3 (Y modes) is always whole.
