public subroutine define_BC_wind_turbine(self)

Boundary Conditions hook (called per substep before transeq). Builds the inlet Dirichlet profile only; outflow parameters are computed in apply_BC from the post-integration velocity. u = bc_start_u + inlet_noise(1) * um * (2r - 1) v = bc_start_v + inlet_noise(2) * um * (2r - 1) w = bc_start_w + inlet_noise(3) * um * (2r - 1)
