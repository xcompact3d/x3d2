public subroutine field_set_abl_wall_stress_base(self, stress, u, w, sample_plane, sample_weight, stress_plane, drag_coeff, component)

Replace one SGS stress plane using the local sampled velocity. The velocity is interpolated linearly between y-vertices sample_plane and sample_plane + 1, with weight sample_weight on the upper one, as Incompact3d samples at a non-integer dsampling.
