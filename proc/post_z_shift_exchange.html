public subroutine post_z_shift_exchange(band, shifted, n_plane, nz_local, z_offset, nshift, z_plane_rank, rank, requests, n_req)

Post the sends and receives of a circular z shift by nshift planes for the planes held by other ranks; band(:, :, k) and shifted(:, :, k) are this rank's local plane k. The tag is the receiving global plane.
