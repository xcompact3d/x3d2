public subroutine read_inflow_block(filename, mesh, u, v, w, times, plane_dt)

Read one block on this rank's y-z subdomain. The arrays are allocated here as (ntimesteps, ny, nz) on the local VERT dims, and times as (ntimesteps). The rank layout may differ from the precursor's, but the global y-z vertex counts must match.
