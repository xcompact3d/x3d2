public pure function quat_rot(v, theta, axis, origin) result(v_rot)

Rotate point v by theta [rad] about axis through origin (right-hand rule). Equivalent to Incompact3d's quaternion QuatRot, written in Rodrigues' form; axis need not be normalised.
