public subroutine estimate_for_num_gpus(ctx, num_gpus, per_gpu_gib, basis, verdict, workspace_gib, overhead_gib, io_gib, used_gib, npeak)

The per-GPU estimate at num_gpus GPUs. The formula floor stands unless the full-grid probe gave a result and the floor is not over the limit. workspace_gib, overhead_gib and io_gib (optional) split the result. used_gib and npeak (optional) are the num_gpus=1 measurement of the real build and its peak_fields: the estimate is then that measurement scaled to num_gpus, and workspace_gib holds the fields and halo alone.
