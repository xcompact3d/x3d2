public pure function predicted_fft_heap_bytes(plan_bytes, worksize_bytes) result(nbytes8)

The NVSHMEM heap of the forward and backward cuFFTMp plans with the workspace allocated and without the xtdesc buffer: the plan memory plus the workspace rounded up to 1 GiB. This is the definition of the measured heap of the normal FFT workspace query.
