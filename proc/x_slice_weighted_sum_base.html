public subroutine x_slice_weighted_sum_base(self, sum_val, f, i_slice, y_weights)

Weighted sum of the x slice f(i_slice, :, :) of a DIR_X field: the point with local y index j counts y_weights(j) times. Gives the flow rate through an x face as the discrete divergence sees it, whose y interpolation weights the boundary planes differently from the interior ones. Caller is responsible for MPI_Allreduce across ranks.
